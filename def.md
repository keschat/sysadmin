## ops

Ops is a versatile abbreviation that stands for Operations or Operational in most workplace, technical, and sports contexts. Its specific meaning depends heavily on the industry or topic:

Platform Ops is the team responsible for curating, maintaining, connecting, and securing the platform that provides Dev, DevOps, SecOps, and (to a lesser degree) NetOps teams with what they need to do their jobs.

**Business & Management (Operations)**

• General Operations: The department or function responsible for coordinating day-to-day business processes, supply chains, logistics, warehousing, and project execution.
• Specialized Ops Roles: Many tech and corporate teams use specialized sub-departments:
	• Sales Ops: Manages CRM software, lead tracking, and sales data.
	• Product Ops: Connects engineering, product design, and go-to-market teams.
	• IT Ops / DevOps / SecOps: Manages server uptime, infrastructure, cloud systems, and security.
	• Local or functional outsourcing providers like Outsourced Project Solutions (OPS) specialize in handling managed warehouse and facility operations.
