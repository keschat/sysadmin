Should I set up standard email accounts? What are they? [stackexchange](https://webmasters.stackexchange.com/questions/2030/should-i-set-up-standard-email-accounts-what-are-they)

Every RIPE LIR (Réseaux IP Européens Local Internet Registry) must have an abuse@ account and based on the RFC 2141 the webmaster@ (http), postmaster@ (smtp) or hostmaster@ (dns) addresses are used by most (all?) Providers.

List of reserved email addresses [Github](https://github.com/collimarco/reserved-email-addresses)
